I learn state representations for biological dynamical systems.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Oslo, working in the GENESIS convergence environment at the Department of Biostatistics. A brain or an embryo is a dynamical system that we only see through noisy, partial measurements, and I build models that infer its hidden state and how that state evolves. The methods come from my PhD on state representation learning, tested on Atari games where the true state is known. I now apply them to brain-computer interfaces and EEG, early-embryo development, and genomics.
