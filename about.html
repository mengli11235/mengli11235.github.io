I build learning systems around the structure of the problem.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Oslo, working in the GENESIS convergence environment at the Department of Biostatistics. My work spans machine-learning algorithms, from self-supervised and reinforcement learning to geometry-aware modeling, and their application to complex biological signals: brain-computer interfaces, early-embryo development, and genomics.
