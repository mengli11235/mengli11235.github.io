AI researcher · University of Oslo

Learning representations that preserve structure.

I study how intelligent systems can discover useful latent structure—from interactive visual worlds to neural and medical data—using self-supervised learning, reinforcement learning, and geometry-aware modeling.

Research question

How can a model learn representations that remain useful when observations are incomplete, labels are scarce, and the underlying structure matters?

Selected research

Three connected directions

Research overview {% include icons.html icon='arrow' %}
01

Self-supervised representation learning

Designing objectives that reveal latent factors without relying on dense annotation—from contrastive prediction to geometry-aware embedding spaces.

02

Reinforcement learning under uncertainty

Studying state, memory, exploration, and visual backbones for agents operating in partially observable and high-dimensional environments.

03

AI for brain & health

Translating representation learning into robust models for neural signals, medical platforms, and early cognitive-health applications.
