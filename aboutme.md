## Introduction

I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Oslo, working in the [GENESIS convergence environment](https://www.uio.no/english/research/strategic-research-areas/life-science/research/convergence-environments/genesis/index.html) at the Department of Biostatistics. My work spans machine-learning algorithms, from self-supervised and reinforcement learning to geometry-aware modeling, and their application to complex biological signals: brain-computer interfaces, early-embryo development, and genomics.
