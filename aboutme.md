## Introduction

I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Oslo, working in the [GENESIS convergence environment](https://www.uio.no/english/research/strategic-research-areas/life-science/research/convergence-environments/genesis/index.html) at the Department of Biostatistics. My work is about learning state representations for biological dynamical systems: inferring the hidden state of a brain or an embryo from noisy, partial measurements, and how that state evolves. I apply these methods to brain-computer interfaces and EEG, early-embryo development, and genomics.
